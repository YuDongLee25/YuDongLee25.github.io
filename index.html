I am a master's student in Intelligent Manufacturing and Robotics at the Southern University of Science and Technology, following a B.Eng. in Automation at South China Agricultural University. My research focuses on robot planning, control, and embodied autonomy. I have worked on semantic object exploration for legged robots, structure-aware generative motion planning for manipulators, and learning- and model-based biped control. Across these projects, I develop planning and control components, evaluate them in simulation, and contribute to integration and validation on real robot platforms.

Selected Research Projects

2025

Semantic Object Exploration for Legged Robots

A decision-driven exploration system for converting natural-language instructions and uncertain semantic observations into stable topological memory and executable subgoals. The work integrates confidence-calibrated perception, controlled-growth semantic topology, semantic utility-based subgoal selection, local planning, and learned locomotion control. I contributed as a co-first author to semantic perception and memory components, planning integration, and closed-loop evaluation in NVIDIA Isaac Sim and on a Unitree Go1 robot.

Paper (opens in a new tab)

2024–present

Cross-Configuration GNN-Diffusion Manipulator Trajectory Planning with Receding-Horizon MPPI-MPC Terminal-Error Recovery

A structure-aware manipulator trajectory-planning framework that represents robot configurations as URDF-derived graphs, encodes robot morphology with a GNN, and uses the resulting representation to condition a Diffusion Model that generates joint-space B-spline trajectories while incorporating differentiable kinematic constraints. A receding-horizon MPPI-MPC terminal-error recovery module addresses residual end-effector error, target updates, joint-limit proximity, and singular configurations. I developed the graph representation, trajectory-generation pipeline, constraint integration, and recovery module, and validated the system across five manipulator configurations in NVIDIA Isaac Sim and on KD7/A10 real robots. Experiments cover cross-configuration trajectory generation, grasp-pose reaching, dynamic target tracking, and receding-horizon terminal-error recovery.

2024–2025

PPO-based Biped Locomotion with Sim-to-Real Evaluation

A point-foot biped locomotion project using PPO in Isaac Gym. Curriculum learning progressively increased task and terrain difficulty, while dynamics randomization covered variations in mass, friction, damping, and observations. I implemented the training workflow, reward and curriculum components, and MuJoCo Sim-to-Sim validation, and contributed to TRON2 deployment and debugging.

2023–2024

Centroidal MPC for Bipedal Walking

An undergraduate thesis project on predictive biped control using a simplified centroidal dynamics model. The controller formulates contact-force and moment optimization as a constrained MPC-QP, incorporates friction and normal-force limits, and combines the optimized contact quantities with swing-leg Cartesian PD control. I implemented the optimization and control pipeline and evaluated walking behavior in MATLAB/Simulink and MuJoCo.
